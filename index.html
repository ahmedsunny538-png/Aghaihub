What this app does

AGHAI VIP Smart Hub is a single-file HTML/CSS/JavaScript dashboard. This page explains the modules in plain language.

ModuleWhat it doesHow it works
Prayer timesFajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha onlyManual city-specific times saved in browser storage
Azan alarmsSeparate alarm toggles and timesBrowser timer; page must remain open, background alarms are not guaranteed
Holy citiesMakkah and Madinah background cardsOnline background photos; custom photos can be selected in Settings
AppearanceAccent, background, border and cell animationSettings stored locally in this browser
Digital clockLive time, Gregorian date and Hijri date where supportedUpdates automatically from the device clock
Inventory / CalculatorStock records, totals, averages and balanceLocal browser storage and CSV export

Important: online photos/audio need internet. Local settings belong to this browser/device. For prayer accuracy, confirm times with your local mosque timetable.
